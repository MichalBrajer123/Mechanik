  https://freesound.org/people/fspera/sounds/528111/

  https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/450873/

  https://freesound.org/people/producerdan/sounds/266626/
  A 12-second section was crossfaded at the loop boundary and set to a quiet background level.
- menu_move_cc0.wav, menu_accept_cc0.wav, menu_cancel_cc0.wav: converted from
  "Menu sounds" by fvcalderan, CC0, respectively menu_change.ogg,
  menu_accept.ogg and menu_cancel.ogg. Levels reduced by 4 dB.
  https://opengameart.org/content/menu-sounds
